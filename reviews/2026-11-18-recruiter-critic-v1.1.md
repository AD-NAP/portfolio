# Recruiter critique: v1.1.0 (Issue 2, the name card)

Run by the `recruiter-critic` subagent on 2026-11-18, on the first card build (before fixes).

**Verdict:** Interview, for a junior to early-mid backend or cloud role (AWS serverless, TypeScript and Python) with an AI-application angle. The card-first opening "hurts a little" as first built: memorable and tasteful, but the first screen gave less than v1.0's cover.

## Concerns and what was done
| # | Concern | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The first screen had no positioning; the title "Systems and Application Engineer" can read as IT support | A one-line intro under the card: "Software engineer: cloud backends and AI tools in Python, TypeScript and AWS" |
| 2 | GitHub, LinkedIn and Resume left the first screen | Real links under the card, beside "Open the build log" |
| 3 | Phones: empty space under the card; a 3-row masthead | The intro and links fill it; the phone contents now fit one row |
| 4 | The cover looked faded mid-flip | Card and cover are pinned together; the grown back swaps instantly for the cover (no faded in-between) |
| 5 | The AI cover line was vague | "An AWS study coach: RAG with citations, Claude tool use, refuses when unsure" |
| 6 | Half-printed section titles while scrolling | The print finishes over a shorter scroll (90% to 72% of the viewport) |
| 7 | Seniority framing | Not a site fix; target junior to mid roles |
