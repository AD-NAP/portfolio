L4Projects

edge-energy-optimizer

Personal project, Oct 2026, in progress

Forecasts a building's energy load a day ahead. The first of five planned phases towards controlling a simulated building, shaving peak demand with a battery, and deploying at the edge.

  • Real meter data from the Building Data Genome Project 2, one office building. Trained on 2016, tested on all of 2017, split by time and never shuffled.
  • Pipeline: naive baselines, calendar, weather and lag features, gradient boosted trees (scikit-learn), evaluation, error analysis, and a forecast() function with tests.
  • Known limits: the model guesses high (bias +13.8 kWh) because the building used about 8% less in 2017 than in 2016, and holidays are the weak spot.

Phase 1 of 5 is done. Next: control logic tested against a simulated building (BOPTEST).

Python, scikit-learn, pytest, uv
